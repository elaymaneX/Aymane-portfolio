## Overview

Built a complete physics-informed neural network pipeline for quantitative OCT image reconstruction. The system jointly recovers tissue attenuation (μ) and reflectivity (r) maps from synthetic confocal B-scans, constrained by a differentiable Beer-Lambert forward model.

## Pipeline

- **Architecture:** Multi-head U-Net in JAX/Flax with separate decoder heads for μ and r, plus an optional PSF branch
- **Stage 1 – Supervised:** Oracle-deconvolution training on synthetic data. Achieved R²≈0.97 for both tissue parameters (RMSE: 0.184 for μ, 0.061 for r)
- **Stage 2 – PINN fine-tuning:** Added a physics-informed reconstruction loss that propagates predicted maps through the forward model. Cut reconstruction log-MSE from 0.89 to 0.24 while maintaining label accuracy
- **Forward model:** Differentiable Tier-1 Beer-Lambert operator with depth-dependent PSF blur, used inside the training loop
- **FDTD simulator:** Built and validated a finite-difference time-domain electromagnetic simulator against analytical Mie scattering theory for future Sim2Sim transfer
